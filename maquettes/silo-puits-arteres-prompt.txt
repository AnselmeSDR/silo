Create ONE coherent game art-direction board with TWO large side-by-side gameplay views of THE SAME underground cylindrical silo. Landscape very wide composition, high resolution. This is an immense vertical cylindrical void, continuous central helical staircase, concentric landings, deep radial inhabited arteries bored into the surrounding rock. Strong spatial depth is the core subject.
LEFT VIEW, about 55% width: title in small restrained French type "LE PUITS CENTRAL". Free camera suspended inside the immense cylindrical central shaft, looking diagonally down and across. Gigantic sheer vertical depth with 25 to 40 repeating curved floor-ring balconies receding down into atmospheric distance, upper rings also continuing beyond frame. A plausible continuous broad metal spiral staircase coils around the inner wall connecting every floor from top to bottom, with railing and visible tiny inhabitants traversing it. NOT a ladder, NOT a set of isolated stair segments, NOT central elevators replacing stairs. Large hollow central void with far curved wall visible, concrete structural rings, aged steel, practical warm lamps. Multiple dark deep openings on each ring lead outward into inhabited radial arteries; visible interior lights and small people hint at actual spaces behind these openings. Camera can move around the void. An unobtrusive vertical level picker at left, selected "Niveau 42". Scale is monumental and functional, hundreds of levels implied, inhabitants tiny relative to architecture. No surface landscape needed. No horizontal array of neighboring silos in this view.
RIGHT VIEW about 45% width: title "UNE ARTÈRE DU NIVEAU 42". Mature sober design, no cartoon mascot, no pixel art, no flat illustration. Less clutter than a cinematic concept painting, architecture and people clearly readable.
